A European-inspired new build on a quiet cul-de-sac, with the finishes still open.

White painted brick, siding accents and steep layered gables give this home a classic presence from the street. Inside, an open-to-above foyer leads to a dining room with a wood-beamed ceiling, and the main level is finished with 10-foot ceilings throughout.

The kitchen centers on a 10-foot island and opens to the living room, where a fireplace and beamed ceiling anchor the space and French doors step out to the covered rear porch. A working scullery, pantry, garage drop zone and powder room keep everyday life running quietly behind the scenes.

Upstairs, the primary suite has its own foyer, a sun room, a bath with separate vanities, tub, shower and enclosed water closet, and a generous walk-in closet. Two more bedrooms each have their own bath, joined by an upper den and the laundry. The third level adds a fourth bedroom suite and a bonus room with a kitchenette and ceilings rising to 12 feet.

Because the home is in the planning stage, early buyers can still help shape the finish selections with the builder.
